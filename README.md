Hi, I'm Abhijeet. Babui Labs is where I do applied LLM work for companies and agencies.
I come from ML research, so I treat every AI system like an experiment: measure it first,
change one thing at a time, and show the number that moved.

### 🪺 Why "Babui"?

The *babui* (baya weaver bird) builds one of the most intricate nests in nature, weaving it
strand by strand until it holds up in any storm. That's how I like to build AI systems:
carefully, one tested piece at a time, made to last.

### 🧵 What I do

| | Service | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 🔍 | **RAG audit & repair** | I measure why your chatbot or search gives wrong answers, then fix retrieval, chunking and prompts, with before/after accuracy numbers. |
| 📏 | **LLM evaluation** | A test suite and report that tell you how accurate, grounded and consistent your AI is, so every change is a measured decision. |
| 🧠 | **Fine-tuning & model selection** | The right-sized model for your task, picked or fine-tuned to cut cost and latency without losing quality. |
| 🔌 | **AI integration** | LLMs connected to your real data and tools (docs, databases, CRMs, APIs) with proper error handling and monitoring. |
| 🛟 | **AI project rescue** | A prototype that stalled or breaks in production, stabilised, tested and shipped. |

### 🛠️ How I work

- **Measured, not guessed:** every engagement starts with a baseline metric and ends with the number it moved.
- **Small milestones:** fixed scope, clear deliverables, no surprises.
- **Your code, your data:** clean, documented handover. Client data is never reused.
- **Research-grade rigor:** held-out test sets, confidence intervals, and honest reporting of what didn't help.
